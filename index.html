◈ How Age Is Calculated

Different cultures calculate age in different ways. This age calculator follows the most commonly used system. In this system, a person's age increases on their birthday. For example, if someone has lived for 3 years and 11 months, their age is counted as 3, and it becomes 4 on their next birthday.

In some cultures, age can be counted in other ways. In certain traditional systems, a person may be considered one year old at birth, and their age increases at the beginning of the new year instead of on their birthday.

Sometimes, the result in months and days can be confusing, especially when dates are near the end of a month. This calculator uses a simple and consistent method to calculate the difference between two dates.

This online age calculator helps you calculate your exact age in years, months, days, hours, minutes and seconds. You can use this birthday age calculator to quickly find your age between two dates.
